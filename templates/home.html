About Nexus

Built for teams

Nexus is an internal AI playground designed to let your team experiment with, validate, and deploy AI-powered tools — without waiting for engineering cycles.

Plug-and-play Applications
Each app is self-contained. Upload data, ask questions, get answers — no setup required for end users.
Private & Secure
All data stays on-premise. Documents are indexed locally with FAISS — nothing leaves your infrastructure.
Modular Architecture
New AI apps can be added independently as Flask Blueprints. The platform scales exactly as your needs do.
